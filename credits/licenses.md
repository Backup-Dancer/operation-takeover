## Operators v3 (`assist/operators-v3` at db74a36)

Operator bodies, skin, eyes, brows, lashes, teeth, tongue, hair, shirts/pants and shoes are generated with **MPFB 2.0.18** (the MakeHuman Community Blender extension) and dressed with assets from the official **makehuman_system_assets** pack. Portrait kit (plate carrier, pouches, pauldrons, helmets, goggles, respirator, hood, role props) is original hard-surface geometry authored in-tree. No community add-on was used. No Quaternius, Sketchfab, Rocketbox, or Mixamo meshes were used.

The MPFB *add-on itself* is GPL-3.0-or-later and is not shipped. MPFB and MakeHuman state that exported meshes and the system asset pack are CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication). Full source log: `assets-src/meta/OPERATOR-MESH-LICENSE.md`. License-page snapshots stay in `assets-src/meta/licenses/`; player-facing markdown copies ship under `credits/licenses/`. Download / snapshot date: 2026-11-09 (America/Los_Angeles).
